01

Brand Voice

Bold, expressive, a little rebellious. Nail’d It! is hand-painted press-on nail art for people who want a statement, not a neutral. The target customer is 18 to 30, finds us on Instagram and TikTok, and loves hand-painted florals, chrome finishes, and bold color.

Short

Say it in a breath.

Headlines are three to six words. Body copy is one or two sentences. If it needs a scroll, cut it.

Confident

No hedging.

“Impossible to ignore,” not “might turn some heads.” We believe in the sets, so the copy does too.

Playful

Wink, don’t shout.

Puns and attitude are welcome (“0 boring details,” “Good Excuses”), but the product stays the hero.

05

The Glow

Glow is the signature, and it is kept consistent: one text glow, one card glow, one logo glow. Script text breathes on a slow 3.2s pulse; marquee items pulse at 2.4s in their own color.

Impress.
Pink text glow

--text-glow-pink: 3, 10, 22, 40px pink layers with a white core. Animated with pink-neon-glow.

HotCoolGold
Color glow

Text shadow in currentColor at 4, 10, 20px, pulsing via ticker-glow. Used in nav, marquee, and chips.

Card glow

--card-glow: dark drop shadow plus 16px pink and 36px violet halos, pink border at 40%.

Logo glow

--logo-glow: drop-shadow 8px pink + 20px violet. Grows a touch on hover.

07

Backgrounds

The page is void black with faint violet light. Sections alternate between glitter black, brick wall, and pattern tile so scrolling has rhythm. Keep two plain sections between repeats of the same texture.

Void

Page base: black with violet glow at top left and a whisper of cyan on the right.

Glitter black

Hero and ritual: tiny sparkles in all five neons, two layers twinkling out of step.

Brick wall

Header, custom orders, gallery. Darkened 30 to 50% so neon reads on top.

Pattern tile

Marquee band and product grid, under a 55 to 78% black veil.

Tie dye

Launch-drop section: soft blooms of pink, cyan, violet, and yellow on near black.

Plum band

Newsletter and contact: #3a193b → #171222 gradient with violet borders.

08

Do & Don’t

Do

  • Put one Sacramento word in every big headline, and make it glow pink.
  • Start every section with a cyan eyebrow.
  • Use real product photos with the pink card border and glow.
  • Rotate the four marquee styles across rows of links or buttons.
  • Keep body copy muted gray so the neon has room to shine.

Don’t

  • Put neon text on a light background, or any mark on white.
  • Fill large areas with flat neon color.
  • Set whole sentences in Sacramento.
  • Stack more than one neon hand mark in the same view.
  • Invent new glow recipes; use the three tokens above.
