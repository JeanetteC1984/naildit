05Open Decisions

What still needs a call before this can go on the Launch Runway checklist.

Simply Stunning price point

24 nails vs. 40+ in the themed sets — needs its own price rather than inheriting the $25 themed-set price.

Zodiac format

One mixed 12-sign set, or a sign-by-sign rotation across the year? Changes both production and the rotation calendar above.

Free gift sourcing per theme

Six different themed gifts means six small-batch sourcing decisions — worth lining up suppliers before the first drop ships.

Reusable packaging design

Shared packaging system across categories, or a distinct box per theme? Affects unit cost and storage.

Subscription vs. one-off

Is Nail Club a recurring subscription (auto-ship monthly) or a themed collection customers opt into each month? Changes checkout, retention, and Marketing planning.

Rotation confirmation

The calendar above is a first pass — confirm against Inventory lead times before it's locked in.
